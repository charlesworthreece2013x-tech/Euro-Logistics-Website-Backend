About Euro Logistics

Euro Logistics is a professional Virtual Trucking Company operating on Truckline MP. Built on respect, realistic driving, and community, we connect drivers across Europe in organized convoys and relaxed daily hauling operations.

Driver Rules & Guidelines

Respect all drivers both inside and outside the company. Toxic behavior, discrimination, or drama will result in immediate removal from the VTC.
Follow all Truckline MP traffic regulations and server guidelines. Maintain realistic driving standards and keep your player tag visible.
Maintain safe braking distance during convoys (minimum 70-100 meters). Follow instructions given by Convoy Control (CC) leads at all times.
