About Euro Logistics

Euro Logistics is a professional Virtual Trucking Company operating on Truckline MP. Built on respect, realistic driving, and community, we connect drivers across Europe in organized convoys and relaxed daily hauling operations.

Driver Rules & Guidelines

Respect all drivers both inside and outside the company. Toxic behavior, discrimination, or drama will result in immediate removal from the VTC.
Follow all Truckline MP traffic regulations and server guidelines. Maintain realistic driving standards and keep your player tag visible.
Maintain safe braking distance during convoys (minimum 70-100 meters). Follow instructions given by Convoy Control (CC) leads at all times.

Legal & Bot Compliance

By inviting or utilizing the Euro Logistics Discord Application within your server or community, you agree to the following terms:

  • The Euro Logistics Bot is designed for VTC management, member logging, and driver operations.
  • Users must not exploit, spam, or attempt to compromise the bot or its API integrations.
  • The management team reserves the right to restrict bot features or access for violations of community rules.

We value the privacy of our drivers and community members:

  • Data Collection: The Euro Logistics Bot only collects standard Discord IDs, server IDs, and voluntary in-game mileage statistics to track VTC progress.
  • Data Usage: Stored data is never sold, shared, or distributed to third parties.
  • Data Removal: Drivers may request complete removal of their logged statistics by contacting Euro Logistics management via Discord.
